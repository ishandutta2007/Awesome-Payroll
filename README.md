## 💻 Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/odoo/odoo?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/stargazers)  
  Comprehensive open-source ERP and CRM with strong HR and Payroll community modules.

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/erpnext?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/stargazers)  
  Full open-source ERP that tightly integrates with Frappe HR, providing accounting, inventory, and broader business context around payroll runs.

- **[Akaunting](https://github.com/akaunting/akaunting)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/akaunting/akaunting?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/akaunting/akaunting/stargazers)  
  Free and open-source accounting software with payroll capabilities designed for small businesses.

- **[Crater](https://github.com/crater-invoice/crater)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/crater-invoice/crater?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/crater-invoice/crater/stargazers)  
  Open-source web & mobile app that helps you manage invoices and basic payments.

- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Dolibarr/dolibarr?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/stargazers)  
  Open source ERP & CRM software for business, featuring employee management and payroll modules.

- **[Frappe HR (formerly ERPNext HR)](https://github.com/frappe/hrms)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/hrms?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/hrms/stargazers)  
  Leading open-source HR and Payroll software built on the Frappe framework.

- **[OrangeHRM (Community Edition)](https://github.com/orangehrm/orangehrm)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/orangehrm/orangehrm?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/orangehrm/orangehrm/stargazers)  
  Popular open-source HR management system covering employee records, leave, time, and performance; often paired with external or custom payroll processing.

- **[IceHrm](https://github.com/gamonoid/icehrm)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/gamonoid/icehrm?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/gamonoid/icehrm/stargazers)  
  Lightweight open-source HRM with employee management, attendance, leave, and basic payroll features suitable for smaller organizations.

- **[OCA Payroll (Odoo Community Association)](https://github.com/OCA/payroll)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OCA/payroll?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/OCA/payroll/stargazers)  
  Collection of open-source Odoo modules for payroll management, salary rules, accounting integration, and related HR extensions.

- **[Custom Laravel / smaller payroll systems](https://github.com/amattu2/payroll-management-system)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/amattu2/payroll-management-system?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/amattu2/payroll-management-system/stargazers)  
  Community projects providing single-tenant employee payroll, timesheets, and time-off management that can be extended for specific needs.

- **[Payroll Engine](https://github.com/Payroll-Engine/Payroll-Engine)** [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Payroll-Engine/Payroll-Engine?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Payroll-Engine/Payroll-Engine/stargazers)  
  Open-source framework for building multi-country, multi-industry payroll applications with configurable regulation layers, REST API, and automation support.

### 🚀 Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Odoo Community HR modules** and Open HRMS builds that extend core Odoo functionality.
- **Country-specific payroll localization modules** (especially strong in ERPNext/Frappe and Odoo ecosystems for India, Europe, and other regions).
- **Calculation engines and rule engines** that can be embedded into larger systems.
- **Self-hosted time-tracking tools** that feed accurate hours into payroll processes.
- Many internal or regional open-source payroll calculators and slip generators.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Start with **Frappe HR + ERPNext** for the most complete open-source HR + payroll experience (especially outside pure US multi-state automated filing). Use **Odoo + OCA Payroll** if already in the Odoo ecosystem. For highly customized or multi-jurisdiction logic, evaluate the **Payroll Engine** framework. Pair any of these with a compliant tax-filing or banking partner where full automation is required.

## 🤝 How to Contribute

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add/edit entries in `README.md` (follow existing format).
3. Include: name, link, 1–2 sentence description, and whether it's SaaS or open-source.
4. Submit PR with a short explanation.

Star the repo if you find it useful!

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Payroll involves legal, tax, and financial compliance obligations. Open-source software can calculate and manage records, but actual tax remittance, filings, and banking integrations often still require licensed providers or careful local configuration. Always consult qualified payroll/tax professionals and verify regulatory acceptance.
- Self-hosted solutions place responsibility for security, backups, data privacy (GDPR, etc.), and accuracy on the organization.
