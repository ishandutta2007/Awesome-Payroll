# Awesome-Payroll

# Top Payroll Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Payroll Processing, Tax Filing, HRIS Integration, Benefits & Global Workforce Payments*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Payroll**. These tools handle employee compensation, tax calculations and filings, direct deposit, benefits deductions, contractor payments, and often broader HRIS functions for domestic and global teams.

**Examples** include Gusto, Rippling, Deel, Remote, ADP Workforce Now, Paychex, Paylocity, Justworks, OnPay, and Square Payroll (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosted HR + payroll systems, calculation engines, and related frameworks — ideal for organizations that want full data control, customization, and no per-employee SaaS fees. (Note: fully automated multi-state US tax filing and banking integrations remain rare in pure open-source solutions.)

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Gusto](https://gusto.com/)**  
  Popular all-in-one payroll, benefits, and HR platform designed for small and medium US businesses, with automated tax filing and employee self-service.

- **[Rippling](https://www.rippling.com/)**  
  Unified workforce platform combining payroll, HR, IT device/app management, and global EOR capabilities in a single system.

- **[Deel](https://www.deel.com/)**  
  Global payroll and Employer of Record (EOR) platform supporting payments, compliance, and hiring in 150+ countries for remote and distributed teams.

- **[Remote](https://remote.com/)**  
  International payroll, EOR, and contractor management platform focused on compliant global employment and payments.

- **[ADP Workforce Now](https://www.adp.com/)**  
  Enterprise-grade HCM and payroll suite from one of the largest traditional payroll providers, covering complex compliance and multi-entity needs.

- **[Paychex](https://www.paychex.com/)**  
  Established payroll, HR, and benefits provider serving small to mid-sized businesses with tax filing, time tracking, and retirement services.

- **[Paylocity](https://www.paylocity.com/)**  
  Modern cloud HR and payroll platform with strong employee experience, analytics, and talent management features.

- **[Justworks](https://justworks.com/)**  
  PEO-style platform that combines payroll, benefits, and HR compliance support, particularly popular with startups and growing teams.

- **[OnPay](https://onpay.com/)**  
  Straightforward, affordable full-service payroll solution for small businesses with transparent pricing and solid core features.

- **[Square Payroll](https://squareup.com/us/en/payments/payroll)**  
  Integrated payroll offering from Square, convenient for businesses already using Square for payments and point-of-sale.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Frappe HR (formerly ERPNext HR)](https://github.com/frappe/hrms)**  
  Leading open-source HR and Payroll software built on the Frappe framework. Includes employee lifecycle, leave/attendance, salary structures, payroll processing, taxation configuration, and mobile app support.

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  Full open-source ERP that tightly integrates with Frappe HR, providing accounting, inventory, and broader business context around payroll runs.

- **[OCA Payroll (Odoo Community Association)](https://github.com/OCA/payroll)**  
  Collection of open-source Odoo modules for payroll management, salary rules, accounting integration, and related HR extensions.

- **[Payroll Engine](https://github.com/Payroll-Engine)**  
  Open-source framework for building multi-country, multi-industry payroll applications with configurable regulation layers, REST API, and automation support.

- **[OrangeHRM (Community Edition)](https://github.com/orangehrm)**  
  Popular open-source HR management system covering employee records, leave, time, and performance; often paired with external or custom payroll processing.

- **[TimeTrex Community](https://www.timetrex.com/)**  
  Open-source time and attendance system with payroll-related capabilities for tracking hours and generating pay data.

- **[IceHrm](https://github.com/gamonoid/icehrm)**  
  Lightweight open-source HRM with employee management, attendance, leave, and basic payroll features suitable for smaller organizations.

- **[Custom Laravel / smaller payroll systems](https://github.com/amattu2/payroll-management-system)**  
  Community projects providing single-tenant employee payroll, timesheets, and time-off management that can be extended for specific needs.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Odoo Community HR modules** and Open HRMS builds that extend core Odoo functionality.
- **Country-specific payroll localization modules** (especially strong in ERPNext/Frappe and Odoo ecosystems for India, Europe, and other regions).
- **Calculation engines and rule engines** that can be embedded into larger systems.
- **Self-hosted time-tracking tools** that feed accurate hours into payroll processes.
- Many internal or regional open-source payroll calculators and slip generators.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Start with **Frappe HR + ERPNext** for the most complete open-source HR + payroll experience (especially outside pure US multi-state automated filing). Use **Odoo + OCA Payroll** if already in the Odoo ecosystem. For highly customized or multi-jurisdiction logic, evaluate the **Payroll Engine** framework. Pair any of these with a compliant tax-filing or banking partner where full automation is required.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Payroll involves legal, tax, and financial compliance obligations. Open-source software can calculate and manage records, but actual tax remittance, filings, and banking integrations often still require licensed providers or careful local configuration. Always consult qualified payroll/tax professionals and verify regulatory acceptance.
- Self-hosted solutions place responsibility for security, backups, data privacy (GDPR, etc.), and accuracy on the organization.
